The Commoditization Shift

Infra/Hosting + Intelligence/Models will be more and more commodity. Reliable/Trustworthy AI will be key: AI that understands and runs end-to-end processes within set boundaries. AI that you can control, own and understand.

05
Opportunities & Threats

Sovereign AI for SAP: opportunity and risk.

Opportunities
1. Sovereign AI becomes a top enterprise AI priority
As AI moves into execution, enterprises must protect process know-how, industry logic and decision patterns.
2. Elevate sovereignty from compliance to competitive advantage
The winning message: scale AI faster without losing control of what makes your enterprise valuable.
3. Differentiate above infrastructure
SAP does not need to own every model or GPU. SAP orchestrates cloud, models, data, agents and processes.
4. Regulatory pressure creates urgency
EU AI Act, GDPR, CLOUD Act concerns, national AI strategies push customers toward clearer control models.
5. Partner ecosystem creates leverage
Delos, Microsoft, European providers, model partners, SIs become repeatable sales motions.
6. Autonomous Enterprise is the long-term prize
SAP knows business data and processes best. Sovereign AI becomes the trust layer for agentic execution.
Threats
1. Hyperscalers define the category too narrowly
If sovereignty becomes only infrastructure, SAP loses the chance to define it as governed enterprise AI execution.
2. Enterprises underestimate strategic IP leakage
Many see AI risk as data privacy only. They may not yet understand the risk of exposing workflows and decision patterns.
3. Customer definitions are fragmented
Data residency, jurisdictional control, operational sovereignty, model sovereignty and air-gapping are often confused.
4. Product readiness must match ambition
Overpromising on air-gapping, local model control, agent governance or auditability would damage trust.
5. Partner complexity can slow execution
SAP's ecosystem is a strength only if converted into clear ownership, sales plays, incentives and pipeline governance.
6. Public-sector cycles are slow
Public sector is strategically important, but regulated enterprise plays must run in parallel.
